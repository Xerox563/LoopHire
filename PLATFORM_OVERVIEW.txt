LOOPHIRE: PLATFORM OVERVIEW & FEATURES

1. WHAT IS LOOPHIRE?
LoopHire is an automation tool that helps you apply for jobs faster. Instead of you spending hours filling out the same forms on different websites, LoopHire uses a smart robot (browser automation) and AI to do it for you.

2. HOW IT WORKS
- You upload your resume once.
- You provide links to the jobs you want.
- LoopHire opens a browser, goes to the job link, reads what they need, and fills out the application using your details.
- It writes a custom message for every job so you don't look like a robot.

3. TOP FEATURES
- Automated Form Filling: No more typing your name and experience 100 times.
- AI Personalization: Writes unique cover letters/messages based on your resume.
- Real-Time Logs: You can see exactly what the bot is doing while it works.
- Review Mode: You can check what the AI wrote before it hits "Submit."
- Job Tracker: Automatically saves every job you've applied to in one place.

4. WHY USE IT?
- Saves hours of manual work every day.
- Allows you to apply to more jobs with higher quality.
- Keeps your job search organized without using spreadsheets.
